Beat the Chill
After a grizzly shredded Jack's clothes, he must race the setting sun to survive the freezing night.
  1. Sprint across the curved earth.
  2. Swing your axe to chop down every last tree.
  3. Collect the fallen wood to use as fuel.
Only by gathering all the wood can he finally spark a blazing fire and survive the chill.
